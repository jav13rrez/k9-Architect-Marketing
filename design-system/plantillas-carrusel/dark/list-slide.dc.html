4 Signs of Trigger Stacking

01
Sudden stillness or freezing The dog locks up mid-motion before escalating.
02
Whale eye / hard stare Prolonged white-eye contact directed at the trigger.
03
Lip licking outside feeding context A displacement signal unrelated to food.
04
Low, sustained growl Often the last warning before a lunge.
