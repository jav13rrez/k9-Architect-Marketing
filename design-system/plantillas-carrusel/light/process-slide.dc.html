The Desensitization Sequence

01
Baseline assessment
Establish trigger distance and behavioral threshold before any conditioning begins.
02
Sub-threshold exposure
Introduce the stimulus at a distance the dog can register without reacting.
03
Counterconditioning
Pair every exposure with high-value reinforcement, timed to the first calm glance.
04
Threshold shift
Close the distance gradually across sessions, never faster than the dog's recovery time.
